// Problem Statement

Manufacturing plants and industrial facilities frequently suffer expensive unscheduled shutdowns because heavy machinery fails unexpectedly under thermal and mechanical stress. Vibration spikes, overheating motor windings, abnormal current draws, and sudden speed fluctuations often develop silently before leading to total equipment failure.

Traditional industrial monitoring systems rely on simple static threshold triggers. As a result, ambient electrical noise or momentary physical knocks produce excessive false alarms, leading operators to ignore alerts altogether.

💡 VibeGuard Solution: VibeGuard AI eliminates false positives through continuous signal processing (Exponential Moving Average & Median Filtering) coupled with a sustained anomaly verification validation engine.

// System Architecture & Data Flow

1. Industrial Telemetry Simulation / Sensors

Multi-parameter sensor streams (vibration, temperature, RPM, current) generated or fed in real-time.

2. Signal Processing & Noise Filtering Engine

Applies Exponential Moving Average (EMA), Median Filter, and Spike Rejection algorithms.

3. Consecutive Reading Anomaly Engine

Evaluates metrics against threshold rules; verifies 3 consecutive abnormal readings before flagging active alert states.

4. Socket.IO Telemetry Router & Node.js Backend

Streams low-latency live telemetry payloads to connected clients; persists anomaly events in MongoDB Atlas.

5. React + Vite Fleet Operator Dashboard

Displays dynamic KPI cards, fleet health overview, dark/light theme options, and live maintenance alerts.
