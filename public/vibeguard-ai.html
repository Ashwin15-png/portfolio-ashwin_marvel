01 — THE PROBLEM

Transient noise causes false alarm fatigue & costly downtime.

// ELECTRICAL & SENSOR NOISE

Single-frame sensor spikes and environmental electrical interference constantly trigger false alerts in standard monitoring systems, leading technicians to ignore critical warnings.

// STATE RENDER BOTTLENECKS

Unthrottled high-frequency telemetry streams freeze browser dashboards and overload database write queues during multi-machine fleet monitoring operations.

02 — THE SOLUTION

Mathematical Noise Filtering & Sustained Anomaly Engine

Engineered a consecutive-reading anomaly validation engine (CONSECUTIVE_READINGS_THRESHOLD = 3) paired with Exponential Moving Average (EMA) and Median filtering. Anomaly alerts escalate strictly when sensor parameters stay abnormal across 3 consecutive cycles.
