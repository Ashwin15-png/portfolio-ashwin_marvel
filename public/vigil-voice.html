01 — THE PROBLEM

The Vulnerability of Modern Voice Channels

Generative AI and voice cloning techniques allow bad actors to generate synthetic speech, impersonate target voices, and perform physical or digital replay attacks with unprecedented realism.

SYNTHETIC SPEECH
Neural text-to-speech models generating human-sounding audio.
VOICE IMPERSONATION
Deepfake voice clones targeting biometric voice authentication.
REPLAY ATTACKS
Pre-recorded audio replayed via loudspeakers or telephony channels.
CORE LIMITATION: Relying on a single detection signal (such as basic spectral thresholding or single-model verification) creates severe vulnerabilities. Robust voice trust requires multi-signal risk fusion across neural anti-spoofing, speaker verification, replay detection, and contextual transaction risk.
