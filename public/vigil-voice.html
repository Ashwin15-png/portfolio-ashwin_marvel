05 — MULTI-SIGNAL TRUST ANALYSIS

Multi-Signal Evaluation Grid

SYNTHETIC EVIDENCE
Evaluates spectral anomalies produced by neural speech synthesis models.
WHY IT MATTERS: Identifies AI-generated voice clones in real time.
SPEAKER EVIDENCE
Evaluates whether the observed voice matches enrolled speaker embeddings.
WHY IT MATTERS: Cloned voices may sound similar but produce inconsistent embeddings.
REPLAY EVIDENCE
Evaluates acoustic artifacts caused by loudspeakers and re-recording devices.
WHY IT MATTERS: Prevents physical replay attacks using pre-recorded clips.
CONTEXT RISK
Evaluates telephony channel metadata, session duration, and frequency patterns.
WHY IT MATTERS: Flags anomalous call originations and sudden channel shifts.
TRANSACTION RISK
Evaluates the sensitivity of the triggered action (e.g., OTP request vs wire transfer).
WHY IT MATTERS: Adjusts policy strictness based on financial exposure.

11 & 12 — ENGINEERING CHALLENGES & MITIGATION STRATEGIES

Technical Bottlenecks & Architectural Mitigations

// CHALLENGE: REAL-TIME LATENCY

Continuous audio analysis must operate quickly enough for live interactions.

→ MITIGATION: Streamlined AI inference pipeline with WebSocket chunking
// CHALLENGE: FALSE POSITIVES

Security systems must balance fraud detection with user experience.

→ MITIGATION: Multi-layer verification & temporal EMA smoothing
// CHALLENGE: MODEL INSTABILITY

Transient frame noise causes unstable frame-to-frame score jumps.

→ MITIGATION: Exponential Moving Average (EMA) smoothing over rolling buffers
// CHALLENGE: MULTI-MODEL INTEGRATION
Running AASIST-L, ECAPA-TDNN, and LFCC-LCNN concurrently.
→ MITIGATION: Multi-signal risk fusion engine & async queue
