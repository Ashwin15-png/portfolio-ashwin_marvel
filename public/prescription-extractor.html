01 — THE PROBLEM

Manual prescription transcription wastes time & causes clinical errors.

// CURSIVE SCRIPT & SHORTHAND

Doctor shorthand, variable stroke widths, and non-standard prescription formats corrupt traditional OCR tools, leading to missing dosage instructions.

// CAMERA SKEW & ILLUMINATION

Mobile phone uploads arrive rotated, shadow-occluded, or low-resolution, confusing standard OCR bounding box classifiers.

02 — THE SOLUTION

OpenCV Preprocessing + Tesseract Scan Engine

Engineered an adaptive computer vision pipeline using OpenCV deskewing, noise reduction, and bilateral threshold filtering paired with Tesseract OCR scanning and fuzzy pharmacopeia dictionary matching to parse 25+ medical fields with high confidence.
